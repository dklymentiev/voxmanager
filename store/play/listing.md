## Full description (≤4000)
```
Voice to PC: Vox Manager turns your phone into a wireless microphone that types on your computer.

Speak into your phone and your words appear as text in whatever window is active on your PC, sent over your local Wi-Fi. No cables, no cloud account, no copy and paste. It is voice to text, straight onto your computer.

WHY A PHONE MICROPHONE
A phone held near your mouth hears you better than a laptop mic across the desk. You can dictate softly in a quiet or shared office and still get clean text on your computer, hands free from the keyboard.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Dictate emails, messages, documents and notes straight into any app on your PC.
- Use your phone as a remote voice keyboard for your computer.
- Speak quietly, up close, instead of typing.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Run the free Vox Manager server on your PC (Windows; macOS and Linux coming).
2. Pair once with a 6-digit code. No QR, no camera.
3. Tap the wave and talk. Your words type themselves on the PC.

PRIVATE AND SECURE BY DESIGN
- Audio is handled by your device's own speech recognition. The app never records audio to files and never sends audio to us.
- Recognised text goes only to the computer you paired with, on your local network.
- Pairing is encrypted: your phone receives a private key one time, gated by a single-use 6-digit code. Every message is signed both ways, so your phone never types into a stranger's computer on the same network.
- No accounts, no analytics, no ads.

Vox Manager is a productivity utility that uses your device's speech recognition to convert what you say into text, then sends that text only to your own paired PC on your local network. See our privacy policy for details.
```
